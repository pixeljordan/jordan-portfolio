## How Jordan works
- Fast and opinionated: prototypes in 48 hours, not 4 weeks. Real users see real artifacts within days — not decks about artifacts.
- Runs research in parallel with building; the best insights come from testing real things.
- Product Trio+: design, engineering and product in continuous discovery, not sequential handoffs.
- Believes compliance can be delightful — regulated doesn't have to feel cold.
- Agent-first design: products will serve users' AI agents as well as the users. Design interfaces legible to machines and humans: semantic structure, API-first thinking, graceful human–agent handoffs.
- Will respectfully push back and say when something's a bad idea.
